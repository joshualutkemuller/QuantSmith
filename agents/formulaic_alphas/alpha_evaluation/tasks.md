# Alpha Evaluation Tasks

## Characterize Alpha

Input: an alpha's signal/return series.

Output: holding period, turnover, and capacity characterization.

## Net-of-Cost Assessment

Input: the alpha and transaction-cost assumptions.

Output: performance net of realistic costs at the alpha's turnover.

## Dependence Analysis

Input: the alpha's returns and reference factors.

Output: volatility and factor-dependence findings, isolating genuine edge.

## Decay & Crowding Review

Input: the alpha's out-of-sample history and known alphas.

Output: correlation-to-known, decay, and crowding assessment.
