# Collateral Management Tasks

## Collateral Allocation

Input: exposures, eligible collateral, and inventory.

Output: a cheapest-to-deliver allocation plan within concentration limits.

## Eligibility & Haircut Review

Input: a financing/derivatives arrangement and its collateral schedule.

Output: eligibility and haircut assessment with corrections.

## Margin Review

Input: initial and variation margin terms.

Output: margin treatment and call-behavior analysis under stress scenarios.

## Rehypothecation & Concentration Review

Input: the collateral pool and reuse terms.

Output: rehypothecation, wrong-way, and concentration risk with mitigations.
