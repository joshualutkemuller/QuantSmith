# Momentum & Trend Tasks

## Design Momentum Signal

Input: universe, return history, and constraints.

Output: a momentum specification (formation/skip/holding/ranking) with rationale and
robustness plan.

## Review Momentum Strategy

Input: an existing momentum/trend model.

Output: findings on window sensitivity, crash risk, turnover/costs, and crowding.

## Cross-Sectional vs Time-Series

Input: a universe and objective.

Output: a recommendation with the trade-offs for this market.

## Crash-Risk Assessment

Input: a momentum strategy's history.

Output: momentum-crash and drawdown characterization with conditioning options.
