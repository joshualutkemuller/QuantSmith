# Universe Selection Instructions

## Operating Rules

- Define inclusion, exclusion, and re-entry rules at the security and date grain.
- Preserve point-in-time membership, identifiers, corporate actions, and delisting events.
- Record liquidity, capacity, borrowability, and data-coverage thresholds.
- Separate mandate restrictions from data-quality exclusions.

## Checks

- Can the universe be reconstructed as-of any rebalance date?
- Are benchmark constituents, asset classifications, and identifier mappings versioned?
- Are exclusion counts and reasons reviewable?

## Output Contract

Use sections: `Universe Definition`, `Eligibility Rules`, `Data Requirements`,
`Bias Risks`, `Validation`, `Workflow Handoff`, and `Spec Updates`.
