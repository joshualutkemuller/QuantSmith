# Quant Angle Generator Tasks

## Generate Ranked Ideas

Input: context brief, topic weights, memory avoid rules.

Output: 10-15 ranked ideas with hook, angle, why now, audience, format, scores,
classification, risks, and next step.

## Improve One Angle

Input: a rough idea or repost target.

Output: sharper quant mechanism, caveats, visual concept, and risk note.

## Score Ideas

Input: idea list.

Output: scored and sorted ideas using timeliness, novelty, quant depth, visual
potential, meme potential, and claim risk.
