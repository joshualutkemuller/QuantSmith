# Market Making & Microstructure Tasks

## Design Market-Making Strategy

Input: venue/instrument microstructure and objective.

Output: a specification with quoting/inventory logic and realistic fill assumptions.

## Review Microstructure Strategy

Input: an existing market-making or order-book strategy.

Output: findings on fill realism, adverse selection, inventory, and latency.

## Backtest-Realism Audit

Input: a high-frequency backtest.

Output: a review of fills, queue position, latency, and same-tick look-ahead.

## Capacity & Impact Assessment

Input: the strategy and target size.

Output: capacity and market-impact characterization with size limits.
