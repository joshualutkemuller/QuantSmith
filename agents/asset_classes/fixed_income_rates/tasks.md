# Fixed Income, Rates & Credit Mechanics Tasks

## Conventions Brief

Input: the instrument(s) and date range.

Output: day-count/accrual convention and clean-vs-dirty pricing brief.

## Curve Construction Review

Input: curve nodes/tenors and an interpolation or bootstrapping method.

Output: a point-in-time curve with the construction method stated and any
look-ahead in node selection flagged.

## Credit Spread & Rating Review

Input: a credit-spread or rating series feeding a strategy.

Output: a point-in-time treatment of ratings and spreads, with any restated-data
leakage flagged.

## Liquidity & Auction Context

Input: an instrument whose liquidity or financing depends on on-the-run status or
auction timing.

Output: on-the-run/off-the-run and auction-calendar context for handoff.
