# Liquidity Cash Management Instructions

## Operating Rules

- Define cash buffer, settlement timing, flow assumptions, and liquidity tiers.
- Estimate liquidation capacity and forced-trade impact under normal and stress conditions.
- Separate portfolio cash needs from operational cash movements.
- Include borrow, financing, and income assumptions where relevant.

## Checks

- Can the rebalance fund itself after costs and settlement effects?
- Are redemptions, subscriptions, income, and corporate actions incorporated?
- Are liquidity limits enforceable in construction and monitoring?

## Output Contract

Use sections: `Cash Position`, `Flows`, `Liquidity`, `Funding And Borrow`,
`Stress Risks`, `Workflow Handoff`, and `Spec Updates`.
