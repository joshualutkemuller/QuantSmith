# Digital Assets Market Mechanics Tasks

## Venue & Price-Discovery Brief

Input: the asset(s) and venue(s) or aggregator source.

Output: a price-discovery methodology brief with cross-venue fragmentation risk
assessed.

## Custody & Counterparty Review

Input: the custody arrangement for a position (exchange, self-custody, qualified
custodian).

Output: a counterparty/insolvency risk assessment for the custody model in use.

## Funding-Rate Mechanics Review

Input: a perpetual-futures position and its funding-rate data.

Output: a point-in-time funding-rate series with the calculation method and
mark-vs-index-price mechanics stated.

## On-Chain & Smart-Contract Risk Review

Input: an instrument with on-chain settlement, oracle, or smart-contract
dependency.

Output: settlement finality, oracle, and smart-contract risk named for handoff to
`risk`.
