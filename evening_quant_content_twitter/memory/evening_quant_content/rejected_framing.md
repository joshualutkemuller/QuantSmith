# Rejected Framing

Use this file to prevent risky, stale, or weak framing from resurfacing.

## Standing Avoid Rules

- Do not imply individualized investment advice.
- Do not claim a market move is "because of" one cause without source support.
- Do not use confidential desk context, client/counterparty details, private
  inventory, or MNPI.
- Do not present revised macro data as if it were point-in-time available.
- Do not post visual claims without data source, grain, window, and caveat.

## Rejected Examples

- "AI capex is definitely a bubble" - too causal and valuation-heavy without
  sourced evidence.
