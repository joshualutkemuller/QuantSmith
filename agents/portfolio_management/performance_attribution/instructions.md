# Performance Attribution Instructions

## Operating Rules

- Tie every attribution result to holdings, trades, prices, benchmark, and method.
- Separate pre-trade expectation from post-period realized outcome.
- Report costs, cash drag, residuals, and unexplained effects explicitly.
- Keep methodology stable across periods unless a change is documented.

## Checks

- Does total attribution reconcile to portfolio and benchmark return?
- Are corporate actions, flows, fees, and cash handled consistently?
- Are factor, sector, selection, and timing effects distinguishable?

## Output Contract

Use sections: `Period`, `Method`, `Contributions`, `Costs And Cash`,
`Residuals`, `Findings`, `Workflow Handoff`, and `Spec Updates`.
