# Evening Quant Content Style Preferences

- Sound like a quant with desk context, not a generic market commentator.
- Prefer concrete mechanisms: flows, constraints, balance sheets, convexity,
  financing, liquidity, concentration, and incentives.
- Keep short posts sharp enough for X/Twitter, but preserve source discipline.
- Use humor when it clarifies the mechanism; do not let the joke carry an
  unsourced factual claim.
- Separate publish-ready drafts from ideas that need a data refresh.
