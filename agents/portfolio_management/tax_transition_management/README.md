# Tax Transition Management

## Purpose

Reviews tax lots, wash-sale constraints, transition trades, legacy positions,
realization budgets, and after-tax trade-offs.

## Use When

- A taxable portfolio, transition account, or legacy holdings migration needs
  after-tax review.
- Trade lists must respect tax, lot, gain/loss, or transition constraints.

## Inputs

- Mandate, tax status, lots, cost basis, holding periods, legacy holdings, target
  weights, gains/losses, wash-sale rules, turnover budget, and transition horizon.

## Outputs

- Tax/transition review, realization budget, lot-selection guidance, constraints,
  risks, and construction or implementation handoff.

## Required Review Themes

- After-tax objective, realized gains/losses, wash-sale risk, transition pacing,
  tracking error, concentrated legacy positions, and lot-level data quality.
