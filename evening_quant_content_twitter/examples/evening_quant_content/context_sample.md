# Sample Evening Context

- Equity concentration remains a useful lens when a broad index is driven by a smaller effective basket of mega-cap weights.
- AI infrastructure spending is easier to debate when capex is compared with free cash flow rather than model-size announcements.
- Repo and collateral discussions are often clearer when framed as balance-sheet capacity and haircut constraints instead of rate forecasts.
- Volatility commentary should separate implied pricing, realized volatility, and the path dependency of hedging flows.
- Market microstructure narratives improve when order-flow, liquidity, and latency constraints are measured separately.
