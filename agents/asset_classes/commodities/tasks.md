# Commodities & Futures Mechanics Tasks

## Conventions Brief

Input: the commodity, contract(s), and date range.

Output: contract specs and physical-delivery-vs-cash-settlement brief.

## Roll Mechanics Review

Input: a continuous futures series and its construction method.

Output: the roll method and roll-date schedule stated, with the roll yield/cost it
implies.

## Curve & Carry Review

Input: front-month vs deferred-month curve data.

Output: a contango/backwardation assessment tied to storage/convenience-yield
economics.

## Delivery & Seasonality Review

Input: a position near contract expiry, or a seasonality-driven signal claim.

Output: physical-delivery risk scoped near expiry, and a point-in-time check on
any seasonality claim.
