# Mean Reversion & Statistical Arbitrage Tasks

## Design Pairs / Stat-Arb Signal

Input: a universe and relationship definition.

Output: a specification with stationarity assessment, thresholds, and cost model.

## Review Stat-Arb Strategy

Input: an existing mean-reversion book.

Output: findings on relationship stability, cost sensitivity, and multiple testing.

## Threshold Design

Input: a spread and its half-life.

Output: entry/exit and stop logic derived from the spread dynamics.

## Regime-Break Assessment

Input: a traded relationship's history.

Output: an assessment of relationship decay and break risk with mitigations.
