# Liquidity Cash Management Tasks

## Define Cash And Liquidity Policy

Input: mandate, flows, holdings, liquidity data, settlement calendar, and funding assumptions.

Output: cash buffer, liquidity tiers, stress assumptions, and limits.

## Review Rebalance Liquidity

Input: target trades, cash, costs, settlement, volume, spreads, and borrow/funding data.

Output: cash, liquidity, funding, forced-trade, and settlement findings.

## Handoff To Construction And Implementation

Input: approved cash/liquidity policy.

Output: optimizer constraints, trade checks, monitoring metrics, and escalation triggers.
