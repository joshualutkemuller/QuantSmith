# Macro & Multi-Asset Tasks

## Design Allocation Strategy

Input: an asset menu and allocation logic.

Output: a specification with macro rationale, point-in-time data plan, and leverage
characterization.

## Review Macro Strategy

Input: an existing macro/allocation/risk-parity strategy.

Output: findings on data vintage, correlation stability, leverage, and regime risk.

## Point-in-Time Macro Data Audit

Input: the economic-data pipeline.

Output: a first-print-vs-revised and release-lag review with corrections.

## Leverage & Drawdown Assessment

Input: the allocation and volatility-targeting rules.

Output: leverage-behavior and drawdown characterization under stress scenarios.
