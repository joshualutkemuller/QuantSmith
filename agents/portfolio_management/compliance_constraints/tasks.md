# Compliance Constraints Tasks

## Map Constraints

Input: mandate, guidelines, restrictions, classifications, holdings, and targets.

Output: measurable rules, source data, owners, thresholds, and enforcement stage.

## Review Breaches

Input: current or target portfolio and constraint map.

Output: pass/fail status, breach severity, exception path, and required approvals.

## Handoff To Construction And Monitoring

Input: approved constraint map.

Output: optimizer constraints, pre-trade checks, post-trade checks, and monitoring fields.
