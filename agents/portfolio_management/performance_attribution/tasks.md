# Performance Attribution Tasks

## Define Attribution Method

Input: mandate, benchmark, holdings, trades, prices, factors, and reporting needs.

Output: methodology, required data, reconciliation checks, and caveats.

## Review Performance

Input: period returns, holdings, trades, benchmark, costs, and factors.

Output: contribution findings, residuals, data issues, and decision feedback.

## Handoff To Research Or Monitoring

Input: attribution review.

Output: signal decay, cost, risk, data-quality, or governance follow-up items.
