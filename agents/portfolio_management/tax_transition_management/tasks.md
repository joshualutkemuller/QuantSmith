# Tax Transition Management Tasks

## Define Transition Policy

Input: mandate, tax status, legacy holdings, lots, target portfolio, and horizon.

Output: realization budget, transition pacing, constraints, assumptions, and risks.

## Review Tax-Aware Trades

Input: proposed trades, lots, basis, holding periods, and restrictions.

Output: gain/loss, wash-sale, tracking-error, lot-quality, and fallback findings.

## Handoff To Construction And Implementation

Input: approved tax/transition policy.

Output: lot-level constraints, objective terms, trade checks, and monitoring fields.
