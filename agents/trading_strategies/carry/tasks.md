# Carry Tasks

## Design Carry Strategy

Input: asset class, instruments, and curve data.

Output: a carry specification with decomposition, financing treatment, and tail
characterization.

## Review Carry Strategy

Input: an existing carry/roll-down strategy.

Output: findings on carry-vs-spot decomposition, tail risk, and financing.

## Tail & Unwind Assessment

Input: a carry strategy's history.

Output: unwind/tail characterization and conditioning or hedging options.

## Financing & Roll Review

Input: the instruments and funding assumptions.

Output: explicit financing, funding, and roll costs netted against the carry.
