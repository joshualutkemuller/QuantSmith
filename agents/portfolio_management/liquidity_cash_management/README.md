# Liquidity Cash Management

## Purpose

Manages cash buffers, subscriptions/redemptions, liquidity tiers, funding, borrow,
income, and forced-trade risk.

## Use When

- Cash, flows, liquidity, funding, or borrow constraints affect portfolio action.
- A rebalance or optimizer needs cash and liquidity assumptions.

## Inputs

- Holdings, target weights, cash, flows, settlement calendar, volume, spreads,
  borrow/funding data, income forecasts, redemption assumptions, and limits.

## Outputs

- Cash and liquidity review, buffer policy, forced-trade risks, funding/borrow
  assumptions, and construction or implementation handoff.

## Required Review Themes

- Cash drag, flow uncertainty, settlement, liquidity tiers, liquidation cost,
  borrow availability, funding cost, and stress redemptions.
