# Universe Selection Tasks

## Define Universe

Input: mandate, benchmark, and available security data.

Output: eligibility rules, exclusion reasons, reconstruction grain, and data contracts.

## Review Universe Bias

Input: universe code, file, query, or spec.

Output: survivorship, liquidity, corporate-action, benchmark, and coverage risks.

## Handoff To Signal And Risk

Input: approved universe definition.

Output: point-in-time universe panel requirements for signal, risk, construction, and monitoring agents.
