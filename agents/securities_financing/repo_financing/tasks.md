# Repo Financing Tasks

## Design Funding Plan

Input: positions to fund or cash to deploy, and collateral.

Output: a repo/reverse-repo plan with rate, term, and haircut treatment.

## Build Funding Curve

Input: repo-rate data across tenors.

Output: a point-in-time GC funding curve with specials distinguished.

## Roll & Term Review

Input: a term-vs-overnight funding mix.

Output: roll-risk assessment and recommendations under stress.

## Counterparty & Haircut Review

Input: the funding arrangement.

Output: counterparty-exposure and haircut/collateral review with mitigations.
