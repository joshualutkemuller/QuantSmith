# Allocation Policy Tasks

## Design Policy

Input: mandate, signals, risk model, benchmark, holdings, cash, costs, and constraints.

Output: sizing rules, risk budgets, baseline, rebalance triggers, and assumptions.

## Review Policy

Input: proposed policy, backtest, or plan.

Output: hidden discretion, fragility, capacity, turnover, factor, and monitoring gaps.

## Handoff To Construction

Input: approved allocation policy.

Output: objective terms, constraints, costs, baseline, and diagnostics requirements for construction.
