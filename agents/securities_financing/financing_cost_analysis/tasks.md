# Financing Cost Analysis Tasks

## Financing-Aware Restatement

Input: a strategy's positions and current backtest returns.

Output: returns restated net of borrow, rebate, funding, and margin cost.

## Cost-of-Carry Decomposition

Input: a financed position.

Output: an all-in cost-of-carry decomposition by leg.

## Financing-Spread Quantification

Input: financing inputs and rate scenarios.

Output: the financing spread and its sensitivity to rates and specials.

## Backtest Financing Audit

Input: a short or long-short backtest.

Output: findings where financing cost is missing or understated, with corrections.
