# Securities Lending Tasks

## Borrow Cost & Availability

Input: securities to short, size, and horizon.

Output: borrow availability and cost (fee, rebate, GC vs specials), point-in-time.

## Financing-Aware Short Returns

Input: a short strategy's positions.

Output: returns net of borrow cost and rebate, with capacity limits from availability.

## Recall & Buy-In Review

Input: a short book.

Output: recall and buy-in risk characterization and mitigations.

## Corporate-Action Handling

Input: loaned/borrowed positions across corporate actions.

Output: manufactured-dividend and voting treatment on the loan.
