# Construction Oversight

## Purpose

Translates allocation policy into optimization-ready objectives, constraints,
costs, diagnostics, and review gates.

## Use When

- Target weights must be produced from forecasts, risk, constraints, costs, and
  current holdings.
- A construction design needs review before routing to optimizer specialists.

## Inputs

- Allocation policy, expected returns, covariance/risk model, benchmark, holdings,
  constraints, costs, cash, liquidity, tax lots, and compliance rules.

## Outputs

- Construction handoff, objective terms, constraints, feasibility checks,
  diagnostics requirements, risks, and optimizer routing.

## Required Review Themes

- Feasibility, objective alignment, turnover, tracking error, factor exposure,
  concentration, liquidity, tax, cost, diagnostics, and rollback.
